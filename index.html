Help

How do I find a lesson?

Open Curriculum, choose a unit, and select a lesson topic.

Why is Preview unavailable?

A lesson needs a configured Google Slides link before its presentation can be previewed.

Why can't someone open a presentation?

Check the Google Slides sharing settings. The viewer needs permission to access the presentation.

Is MathWord the official Bridges website?

No. MathWord is an independent fan-made educational project. Bridges in Mathematics is a program of The Math Learning Center.
